One structured learning space

Everything has a purpose.

The site is designed for regular independent preparation, not passive browsing. Each section helps you produce something you can reuse aloud, discuss with a teacher or test in a real colle.

M

Methodology

Reading and listening strategies, contextualised introductions, relevant key questions, coherent plans and effective conclusions.

C

Civilisation

Country knowledge, current issues, timelines and the context needed to support convincing arguments.

V

Vocabulary

Useful, precise English with revision tools, games and opportunities to retrieve and reuse new language.

G

Grammar

Clear explanations and active practice to build structures you can use spontaneously rather than merely recognise.

P

Pronunciation

Sounds, stress and intonation practice focused on clarity, confidence and intelligibility.

T

Timelines

Turning points, continuities and connections across the English-speaking world.

R

Resources

Reliable articles, podcasts, videos and sources for further reading and listening.

J

Jury reports

Examiner expectations, recurring advice and practical lessons drawn from official reports.

How access works

A simple, controlled route into the private site.

The learning area is not publicly available. Requests are reviewed manually so that access remains personal and the site can be reserved for approved users.

1

Send a request

Complete the short access form with an email address you can check.

2

Wait for review

The site administrator reviews each request individually.

3

Create your password

Approved users receive a secure invitation by email.

4

Enter the learning space

Sign in with your approved account and begin working independently.
